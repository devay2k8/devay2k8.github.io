PanelFlow Reader Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026. This policy applies to the PanelFlow Keyless Dialogue Lab Android app (com.pdnexus.panelflow.autodialoguelab). Envisioned by PD Nexus. IDEAS • APPS • IMPACT.

Information handled by the app

PanelFlow reads files you choose to import and keeps private copies, library metadata, reading positions and history, bookmarks, notes, settings, and optional dialogue and voice-cast choices on your device. On-device OCR may process pages you choose to recognize. The app does not require a PanelFlow account and has no app-added advertising or analytics workflow.

Optional sharing

Local reading does not require the app to upload books. If you connect an Android document-provider cloud folder and enable sync, the app reads books from that folder and writes a reading-progress file to it. Uploading book files is a separate setting that is off by default. The selected cloud provider controls its own storage and retention.

If you opt in to online speaker suggestions and enter your own OpenAI API key, the app sends the manga title for cast lookup, then the selected page image and OCR dialogue text for speaker matching over HTTPS to OpenAI. Continuous online playback can send later pages. The key is held only for the current reader session by this app and is sent in request headers. The app requests store: false; OpenAI's own terms and policies govern its handling. Do not enable this feature for material you lack permission to transmit.

Support & feedback opens your email application addressed to pdnexus.apps@gmail.com. The app does not send feedback itself. Messages you choose to send are handled by email providers and the support recipient.

Permissions and device services

Internet and network-state permissions support optional online operation. Broad shared-storage access is requested only through a separate Android approval for automatic Downloads scans; individual file import remains available through Android's picker. The app uses installed Android text-to-speech voices and an on-device OCR component. Device and third-party providers may have their own privacy practices.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Local app data remains until deleted with available app controls or Android's app-data or uninstall controls. Clearing reading history or a prepared cache does not delete the complete library. Exported backups are unencrypted JSON containing settings and library metadata, including notes and positions, but not book files; you control their location and deletion. Cloud copies, progress files, and sent email are outside the app and must be deleted through the respective provider. PanelFlow does not maintain a server-side copy of the local reading library.

Security and choices

Imported copies use Android app-private storage; Android backup is disabled in this build. Optional online requests use HTTPS. Protect exported backups and API keys. You may keep online suggestions disabled, disconnect a cloud folder, disable book uploads, or contact us with privacy questions.

Contact

Support: pdnexus.apps@gmail.com. Contact us about privacy or deletion of feedback sent directly to us; provider-held material must also be handled with that provider.
