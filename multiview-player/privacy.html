Videos and local access
MultiView Player plays videos you select through Android's system document picker. It reads those selected files or document-provider streams for playback and displays their file identifiers in the current session. It does not scan your media library, modify or delete your original videos, or upload them. Providers you choose, including cloud providers, may use their own network services and policies.

Storage and retention
The app does not create accounts or a media database. Playback positions, per-video volume, speed and per-video fit/zoom choices are held for the current activity session; they are not saved as a viewing history. Interface preferences (theme, button labels, size, shape, control-bar appearance and button visibility) are saved privately on your device until you reset them, clear app storage or uninstall. Reset to defaults clears only these interface preferences, not original videos or the running playback session. Android can retain read-access grants for documents you select. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its app data and access grants. This does not delete original videos. Android backup and device services operate under your device settings and their own policies.

Collection, sharing and third parties
The application declares no Internet permission. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account, AI or crash-reporting service. It does not automatically send playback data or personal information to PD Nexus. Android framework and AndroidX/Material user-interface libraries provide playback and interface functions. System diagnostics and services are governed by Android and your device provider.

Feedback and support
Feedback is optional. You choose a category, message, optional reply address, and whether to include Android version and CPU architecture. The app opens a reviewable draft in your email app; it does not send it automatically. If no email app is available, it copies the draft to your clipboard and tells you. Your email and clipboard providers may handle that content under their own policies, including device clipboard sync if enabled. Information you choose to email is received at pdnexus.apps@gmail.com and used to respond and investigate your request. Do not include private videos or secrets. Support correspondence is retained only as necessary to address the request and applicable obligations; contact us to request access, correction or deletion.

Online privacy policy
Opening the online policy uses your chosen browser. The policy is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub and your browser may process ordinary connection information under their own policies. The complete policy is also available inside the app without a network connection.

Security and your choices
Android's application sandbox and document permissions restrict access. No security measure is absolute. You control selected files, optional feedback and device permissions. You may stop using the app or clear app data at any time. Contact the support address for privacy questions and applicable access, correction or deletion requests.

Children and changes
The app does not ask users for age or knowingly collect children's personal information. Target-audience and content-rating declarations must be set by the publisher in Google Play Console. We will update this policy when the app's data handling changes.
