NEWSORBIT · PRIVACY

Your news. Your device. Your choices.

Privacy Policy · Updated 23 October 2026

This policy describes the NewsOrbit 0.1.0 alpha implementation, not planned future features. Android build, device testing and release validation are not complete. NewsOrbit is not being represented as a released or Play Store-approved application.

1. Overview

NewsOrbit is a local-first Android news reader from PD Nexus. No registration, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, paid AI service or NewsOrbit cloud backend is included. NewsOrbit uses publisher-supplied feed excerpts and links readers to original reports; it does not bypass paywalls or scrape full articles.

2. Information stored on your device

Selected topics, source choices, optional manually entered region interests, custom RSS/Atom feeds, article metadata, publisher-supplied excerpts, bookmarks, optional reading/search history, source-check status and notification records are stored in app-private storage. Prepared speech is cached locally. These records are not sent to PD Nexus.

3. Connections to news and image providers

Refreshing connects directly over HTTPS to selected feed servers. Their operators and hosting providers can receive ordinary connection information, including your IP address, request time, requested feed URL and the app's user-agent. With article images enabled, image hosts receive requests too. Wi-Fi-only and data-saver settings are available. Source operators have their own privacy policies and terms.

Source blocks and selected-only rules apply to the reader, local search, summaries, briefings and alerts. A saved item can remain stored but hidden by your current source rules; deleting all local data removes it. Source links and automated grouping do not certify factual accuracy or independent verification.

4. Summaries and audio

Briefs use local extractive sentence selection, not a cloud language model. Optional speech uses an installed Android text-to-speech voice marked as offline. Voices requiring a network connection are rejected. Voice installation and the installed speech engine are managed by Android or its provider and may have separate privacy practices. Playback pauses when the app leaves the foreground. No microphone recording is used.

Large generative models, direct X data collection, Google News integration, video summaries and translation are unavailable in this alpha. They are not silently routed to paid or cloud services.

5. Permissions

Internet and network-state permissions support feed retrieval. Notification permission, where Android requires it, is requested only when notifications are enabled. NewsOrbit does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or unrestricted storage access. Optional regions can be entered manually. Import/export uses Android's document picker. Automatic refresh is optional and may be delayed by Android.

6. Feedback, external links and exports

Support opens your email application with a draft. NewsOrbit does not send a message itself or claim that it was delivered. Basic app/device details are included only when you select that option; reading history and feed lists are not attached automatically. Your email service processes information you choose to send.

Opening original articles, privacy links or voice settings hands control to external applications and providers. Exported preference JSON and WAV audio are saved to a location you select. A chosen cloud document provider may upload those files under its own policy. NewsOrbit does not control exported files or sent emails.

7. Retention and deletion

Unsaved news is pruned during refresh after approximately 14 days and bounded to a recent cache. Saved article text is retained until you remove it or delete all local data. Reading/search history can be disabled and cleared. Clear Cache removes unsaved news, images and prepared audio while retaining saved story text. Delete All Local Data removes the app's stored content and settings. Uninstalling removes app-private data. Exported files and emails must be deleted separately.

Android app backup is disabled in the manifest; device manufacturers' transfer behavior may vary.

8. Security and important limitations

The implementation uses HTTPS, bounded XML parsing, rejection of external entities and restrictions on network destinations. These controls reduce risk but do not guarantee perfect security. Android device, security, accessibility and release checks still need to be completed.

News can contain errors, disputed claims and mature subject matter. Do not rely on summaries as emergency, medical, financial or legal advice. Consult the original source and appropriate official or professional guidance for high-stakes decisions.

9. Children and changes

NewsOrbit is a general-news reader, not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request children's personal information. This policy will be updated if the implementation changes. Any future feature that sends data to a service must be disclosed before it is enabled.

10. Contact

For questions or privacy requests, contact pdnexus.apps@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
